# Clinician workspace

Mode: Operate. Scope: frontend/app/src and clinician workflows.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A minimalist clinician workspace makes the next task obvious. No decorative dashboard cards or charts.

OWN-WORLD: White content, gray navigation, one blue action accent, Segoe UI, fine dividers, compact patient lists. These follow the user's explicit minimalist brief.

STORY: Review today's appointments and unfinished notes; open a patient; document the visit; save or sign it.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A narrow left navigation, compact search header, Today heading and actions, appointment rows at left, drafts and patient shortcuts at right. Mobile uses a top navigation and stacked work areas. Primary actions remain adjacent to the current task.

FORM: Patient-first workspace; user-pinned minimalist direction overrides decorative alternatives. seed:a24710a4de4cc590. Signature interaction: continue an unfinished SOAP note while keeping patient allergies and conditions in context. Code-led app surface; no approved image comp.
